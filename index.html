The Story

Ancient Sounds · Modern Beats

Born and raised in Nazareth — one of the oldest cities on earth — John Lawen grew up surrounded by the rich textures of Middle Eastern music, its deep rhythms and haunting melodies woven into everyday life.

At just 17 years old, he stepped behind the decks and never looked back. What started as a teenage obsession quickly became a mission: to bridge worlds, blending the pulse of Melodic House and Tech House with the soulful resonance of Arabic and Middle Eastern tradition.

Today, DJL performs across clubs, festivals, and online stages, delivering sets that don't just move bodies — they move cultures. Every mix is a dialogue between past and present, East and West, ancient and electric.
